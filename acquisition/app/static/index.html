Tag analysis

Fills tracks that have no BPM, key, genre, year or mood. Genre and year come from MusicBrainz first, then Discogs and Last.fm; when they have nothing, genre, mood, BPM and key are estimated from the audio. Values already in a file are kept, except that keys are rewritten as Camelot (Am becomes 8A) and genres in one spelling (Hip-Hop becomes Hip Hop). Key tags that are not keys are listed under Key → Not a key for you to fix. BPM and key are not estimated for files longer than 10 minutes; genre and mood are not estimated from audio beyond 20 minutes.
